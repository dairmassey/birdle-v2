Accessibility

#171717 text passes WCAG AA on #a8d03a, #f8c97c, #addcf5 and #e6e6e6, and is about 4.6:1 on Glimpse #e24e40. Correct (lime) and present (peach) now differ mostly by hue. Run a color-blind check and keep the state in each tile's and key's aria-label.
